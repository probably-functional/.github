# Probably Functional

> We make bullshit that’s useful.\
> It probably already exists — ours is obviously better. And probably functional.

## Why “Probably Functional”?

Because our software works **50% of the time**, all of the time.

The other 50% is a valuable learning experience.

## What we do

We build small, slightly unhinged tools that solve problems you may or may not have had.

- Useful enough to keep
- Chaotic enough to enjoy
- Documented just enough to be dangerous

## Principles

- **Ship it.** Perfection is for people with fewer deadlines.
- **Make it useful.** Even if the use case is questionable.
- **Break things carefully.** Preferably in a branch.
- **Laugh at the bugs.** Then fix them. Probably.
